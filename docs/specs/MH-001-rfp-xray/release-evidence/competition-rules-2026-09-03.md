# Monid “We Kill” competition rules capture

Captured: 2026-09-03 UTC.

## Sources

- Official competition page: https://hacks.monid.ai/
- Official submission guide: https://hacks.monid.ai/guide.html

This record paraphrases the public pages. Recheck all three sources before
registration, publication, and final submission because the organizer may
change operational details during the event.

## Confirmed deadlines and eligibility

- Registration closes September 9, 2026.
- Competition submissions close September 10, 2026 at 23:59 ET.
- Teams may contain up to three people and use one bound social handle.
- The judged Monid integration and replacement workflow must be new work from
  the ten-day event; prior foundations are allowed, and the commit history must
  show the new work.
- The registration form requires a team name, Member 1 name and email, and the
  exact email on the entrant's Monid account. Member 2 and Member 3 are
  optional. The competition key and credits are attached to that Monid account.

Registration is an identity-bearing external submission. It must not be filled
from Git metadata or guessed profile information.

## Product and evidence requirements

- The product must run on live data and produce the workflow output a customer
  buys, including bad-input, empty-result, and rate-limit behavior.
- The submission must show a measured run cost, including failed runs. An
  estimate is not an acceptable substitute.
- The competitor must have a current public price. The comparison must identify
  the workflow replaced and avoid implying replacement of unimplemented
  features.
- The Monid integration must be visible in the demo, including actual discovery
  or call evidence and its cost readout. A logo or tutorial wrapper is not
  sufficient.

## Video and distribution requirements

- The official checklist requires the video to be under 90 seconds. The guide
  recommends 60–90 seconds, with the outcome clear during the first five
  seconds and most of the runtime showing the product working.
- The video needs captions and should be exported at 1080p or higher.
- X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube each award the first platform post
  50 reach points. Native video is required by the platform guidance; X should
  not be only a YouTube link.
- Every platform post must contain `#monid`. Each published URL must be
  registered within 24 hours. At least one registered post is required for a
  valid submission.
- Instagram and TikTok require a vertical 9:16 cut. YouTube may use a Short or
  a longer walkthrough, but the registered competition video still must follow
  the final submission checklist.

## Submission fields described by the guide

Alongside the repository or live URL, prepare:

1. **What you killed** — one sentence naming the product, current price, and
   workflow replaced.
2. **How you used Monid** — the real endpoints, execution order, and result that
   was otherwise unavailable.
3. **The receipt** — actual measured cost, including failed runs.
